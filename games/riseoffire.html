Rise of Fire

Your realm


Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom. Click one of your armies (round markers) to select it, then click any tile to march there. Armies cross seas, but cannot enter another realm’s land until you declare war or ally with it. Recruits appear in your capital (gold star ★). If an enemy captures your capital, your treasury is halved, your armies lose 25% strength and health until you retake it, and you cannot recruit. “Train troops” takes 12 seconds per level: 5 levels, each giving +15% health, speed and strength, and the levels stack. Use “Nation layer” (or press N) to hide the nation colours and see the real Earth underneath.
